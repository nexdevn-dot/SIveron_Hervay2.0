# Welcome to the Siveron wiki

Siveron is a locally operated **B2B research and outreach workspace**. It brings
company discovery, evidence-based signals, reviewed contact research, company
reports and approval-based campaigns together in a German/English dashboard.

Developed by **Bao Dang**, based on **Harvey by Ethan Rogers**. Application code
and local modifications remain MIT-licensed; third-party licenses are preserved.

## Development defaults

The public template contains no business configuration or service credentials.
Email and LinkedIn are disabled, the daily sending limit is zero, approvals are
required and the initial database sending switch is paused. Installing or opening
the dashboard does not authorize outreach.
